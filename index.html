Chapter 07 — The fine print

Questions,
answered.

What is degen.exchange?

An AMM-based decentralized exchange on Robinhood Chain. Swap tokens straight from your wallet — no sign-up, no account.

What is Robinhood Chain?

An EVM-compatible Layer 2 built on Arbitrum technology, designed for tokenized assets and DeFi. degen.exchange is an independent project running on that network and is not affiliated with Robinhood.

Which wallets are supported?

Any EIP-1193 compatible EVM wallet, including Robinhood Wallet, MetaMask, Rabby and Coinbase Wallet.

What does it cost?

A 0.25% fee per swap (0.20% to liquidity providers, 0.05% to the protocol), plus a very small network gas fee.

Are my funds safe?

degen.exchange is non-custodial: we never hold your funds or ask for your seed phrase or private keys. Never share your seed phrase with anyone.
